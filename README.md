# State of Data Brazil

State of Data Brazil is an annual survey and dataset produced through a partnership between Data Hackers, the largest data community in Brazil, and Bain & Company, a global consulting firm, aimed at mapping the Brazilian data job market. The 2022 edition, conducted between October 10 and November 28, 2022, gathered responses from 4,271 professionals across the country working in roles such as data analyst, data scientist, and data engineer, spanning experience levels from junior analysts to managers. The survey covers a wide range of indicators, including demographic profile, educational background, industry sector, compensation, turnover, and workplace satisfaction factors, including the impact of remote work on professional preferences. The resulting dataset, available on Kaggle, offers one of the most comprehensive snapshots of the data industry workforce in Brazil, and a full report with additional analysis and insights is also available on the survey's official website.
